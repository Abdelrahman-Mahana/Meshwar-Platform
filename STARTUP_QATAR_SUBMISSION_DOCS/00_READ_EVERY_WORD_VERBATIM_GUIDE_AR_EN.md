### النص المكتوب حرفياً:

>
> **Subtitle:** `No money sits in anyone's private pocket mid-task. MASHWAR Escrow holds funds until every proof step clears.`

| Escrow Step | Protection Guaranteed for Both Parties |
| --- | --- |
| Step 1: Free Post (0 QAR) | Requesters post any task, site supply, or ride at 0 QAR upfront. No charges apply before offer acceptance. |
| Step 2: Bilateral Lock | Upon acceptance, service fee (e.g., 45 QAR) + store cap (e.g., 180 QAR) lock inside MASHWAR Escrow via Fawran, QPay, SkipCash, or Wallet. |
| Step 3: Live Receipt Check | Provider uploads in-store photo + paper invoice (#QA ref). Requester approves actual cost before dispatch. |
| Step 4: GPS & Masked Chat | Live Qatar route tracking and in-app chat without exposing personal phone numbers to strangers. |
| Step 5: OTP or 1-Tap Freeze | Funds release only after entering the 4-digit handover OTP. Either side can freeze payout in 1 tap for admin audit. |
