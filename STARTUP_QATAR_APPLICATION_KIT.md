### Q2. What exact problem are you solving, and why do existing solutions fail?

> Inter-city movement of people, urgent documents, and small commercial stock across Egypt and comparable regional corridors suffers from four structural failures that neither urban ride-hailing apps nor legacy couriers solve:
>
> 1. **Uneconomic Inter-City Coverage:** Urban ride-hailing platforms (Uber, Careem, inDrive) price trips as dedicated single-party charters, making 60–250 km inter-city or industrial-zone trips cost-prohibitive for daily commuters, university students, and technicians—while outer towns and industrial zones suffer low supply availability.
> 2. **Underutilized Private Vehicle Capacity:** Tens of thousands of private sedans and light pickups commute daily along fixed inter-city highways carrying one driver, three empty seats, and an empty trunk despite rising fuel, toll, and maintenance costs.
> 3. **Slow, Hub-Bound Courier Latency for Urgent Micro-Freight:** Traditional parcel networks require branch drop-off, central sorting, and next-day dispatch (24–72 hours minimum). A clinic needing lab supplies, a car workshop needing a spare part from a central market, or a company needing stamped documents cannot wait 48 hours, yet hiring a dedicated courier car is financially irrational.
> 4. **Unverified Informal Coordination Risk:** Users currently resort to ad-hoc social media and messaging groups to arrange rides and parcel handoffs. Those informal channels lack identity verification, price guardrails, chain-of-custody OTP proof, live location tracking, phone privacy, and dispute resolution—creating measurable safety and fraud exposure.

### Q3. Describe your product and how each core component works together.

> MASHWAR resolves supply fragmentation by uniting passenger seats, parcel trunk space, and errand execution on the same underlying route graph so driver utilization and passenger liquidity reinforce one another:
>
> * **Corridor-Aware Matching Engine:** Using `GoogleMapsService` (Places Autocomplete, Geocoding, Directions API) backed by a built-in deterministic Geo-Catalog covering all 27 Egyptian governorates as an offline fallback, the system filters rides along a driver's actual polyline and configurable proximity buffer (default 3,000 meters).
> * **Atomic Booking & Instant/Approval Workflows:** Drivers publish routes with fixed seat pricing constrained within ±25% of reference fuel-cost baselines to prevent surge gouging. Riders book via instant confirmation or submit requests subject to a driver response SLA (default 30 minutes). Seat inventory updates execute inside atomic Cloud Firestore transactions to prevent double-booking race conditions.
> * **OTP-Verified Parcel & Document Handoff:** Drivers opt into parcel transport on existing trips with explicit trunk weight limits (15 kg default) and transparent size tiers. Senders upload item photos, acknowledge prohibited-item rules, and receive a cryptographic/numeric pickup-and-delivery OTP. Neither settlement nor delivery completion finalizes until the OTP matches at handoff.
> * **Dual-Intent Marketplace Board:** Any verified user can post a structured request or travel offer across four categories (Carpool, Shopping/Procurement, Official Paperwork Errands, and SME Merchant Freight). Providers submit competitive priced offers tied to their verified profiles and travel schedules.
> * **Safety, Privacy & RBAC Administration:** All users pass identity checks before transacting on sensitive flows. In-app messaging keeps phone numbers masked, active trips allow live route sharing with up to three emergency contacts and direct SOS dialing, and a separate Admin Web Control Panel (`lib/admin_main.dart`) equips compliance and operations staff to review KYC queues, resolve parcel disputes with audit trails, and adjust platform risk limits live.

### Q6. Business Model & Unit Economics

> MASHWAR operates an **Asset-Light Multi-Vertical Marketplace** with **zero vehicle fleet CapEx**, **zero full-time driver payroll**, and **zero physical warehouse leases**:
>
> * **Core Monetization (Dynamic Category Commission):**
>   * The platform takes a configurable percentage commission on each completed transaction (carpool seat booking, parcel delivery, or completed community board task), dynamically configurable per vertical from the Admin Control Panel.
>   * Lower take-rates on commuter carpooling drive daily driver supply and corridor density (customer acquisition engine), while higher-value **same-day parcel delivery** and **merchant/SME logistics** carry higher take-rates commensurate with 2–4 hour delivery speed.
> * **Payment Architecture & Local Financial Inclusion:**
>   * Designed to support both **cash settlements** (via driver prepaid commission/escrow wallet ledger to prevent cash-leakage churn) and **local digital wallet rails** (supporting bank/instant-transfer top-ups and receipt verification workflows, ready to integrate regional payment gateways such as **QPay / Sadad / SkipCash / Apple Pay / Cards** in Qatar alongside **InstaPay / Vodafone Cash / Fawry** in Egypt).
> * **Future Revenue Expansion Layers (Post-PMF):**
>   1. **B2B Corridor Subscriptions (Mashwar Business):** Monthly plans for clinics, e-commerce sellers, workshops, and legal offices requiring guaranteed same-day inter-zone dispatch and consolidated invoicing.
>   2. **Optional High-Value Cargo Protection:** Micro-fee coverage tier for declared parcel values above the standard platform threshold.
>   3. **Priority Request Boosting:** Paid urgency placement for time-critical board requests.
> * **Why Unit Economics Compound Over Time:** One acquired user cross-transacts across three roles (rider today, parcel sender tomorrow, errand client next week), amortizing CAC across multiple transaction verticals while dual-load drivers (passengers + parcels on the same highway trip) achieve higher net earnings per kilometer without raising prices for end users.

### Q13. Key Operational, Technical & Regulatory Risks & Mitigations

| Risk Category | Specific Failure Mode | Engineered / Operational Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regulatory & Licensing** | Misclassification between commercial taxi/courier licensing and peer-to-peer cost-sharing / vetted parcel transport in Qatar & Egypt. | Structure Qatar legal classification upfront via QFC/QSTP legal counsel; enforce fuel-cost price caps (±25% guardrail) on passenger cost-sharing; keep B2B commercial parcel runs restricted to verified vehicles compliant with local commercial transport permits. |
| **Cold-Start Liquidity (Chicken-and-Egg)** | Empty corridors fail to match riders/parcels on time, causing immediate user churn. | Launch **corridor-by-corridor**, never nationwide on day one; combine passenger seats + parcels + merchant errands on the same driver trip to boost per-trip utilization; deploy route-alert notifications so latent demand notifies drivers immediately. |
| **Trust, Safety & Cash-Leakage Fraud** | Fake driver accounts, parcel substitution/theft, or drivers skipping platform commission on cash trips. | Mandatory 14-digit National ID / QID + license review before activation; OTP pickup & delivery proof; prohibited-items enforcement + photo proof; prepaid driver commission balance requirement blocking new rides if negative commission threshold is exceeded. |
| **Operational Map / Cloud Cost Overruns** | Uncontrolled Google Maps Autocomplete / Directions API calls eroding unit margins per search. | Debounced autocomplete queries, local geospatial hub caching (`Egyptian Geo-Catalog` + Qatar hub catalog), and backend caching of corridor polylines and highway distances. |

## القسم الخامس: هيكل النموذج المالي المطلوب لـ QDB (3-Year Financial Model Blueprint)

لأن لجان بنك قطر للتنمية (QDB) تدقق في اتساق الأرقام بين نموذج التقديم وملف الـ Excel، يجب بناء الملف من **6 تبويبات مترابطة بالمعادلات** (لا أرقام مكتوبة يدوياً في النتائج):

1. **`1_Assumptions` (مدخلات الفرضيات):**
   - عدد الممرات النشطة شهرياً (الأشهر 1–36: بداية بممر واحد ثم التوسع التدريجي).
   - السائقون الموثقون النشطون/ممر، متوسط الرحلات الأسبوعية/سائق، متوسط المقاعد المحجوزة/رحلة، ومتوسط الطرود المرفقة/رحلة.
   - متوسط سعر المقعد ومتوسط رسوم الطرد + نسبة العمولة (`Take Rate %`) لكل فئة (مقترح: `10–12%` للسفر التشاركي، `15–20%` للطرود والمصالح الفورية).
   - تكلفة الاستحواذ (`CAC`) للسائق وللمستخدم + معدل الاحتفاظ الشهري (`Retention %`).
2. **`2_Volume_and_GMV` (الحجم التشغيلي الشهري — 36 شهراً):**
   - `Completed Rides = Active Drivers × Trips/Driver × Utilization`
   - `Completed Parcels & Board Tasks = Corridor Volume × Attach Rate`
   - `Total GMV = (Seats × Avg Seat Price) + (Parcels/Tasks × Avg Delivery Fee)`
3. **`3_Revenue` (الإيرادات وصافي الحصة):**
   - `Platform Gross Revenue = SUM(Category GMV × Category Take Rate) + B2B Subscriptions (Months 13+)`
   - خصم الحوافز الترويجية (Driver/Promo Rebates) للحصول على **Net Revenue**.
4. **`4_COGS_and_Tech` (التكاليف المباشرة لكل معاملة):**
   - تكلفة `SMS OTP` لكل مستخدم جديد/حجز، رسوم بوابات الدفع الإلكتروني (`Payment Gateway %` على المعاملات غير النقدية)، تكلفة `Google Maps API` لكل طلب بحث بعد الكاش، وتكلفة Firebase/Cloud Functions لكل معاملة.
   - هذا التبويب يثبت للجنة أن **هامش المساهمة الإجمالي (Contribution Margin)** إيجابي لكل معاملة حتى مع زيادة الحجم.
5. **`5_Team_and_Doha_OpEx` (فريق العمل ومصاريف التشغيل في قطر):**
   - رواتب المؤسسين والمهندسين وفريق مراجعة الهويات KYC وتطوير الأعمال في الدوحة (بالريال القطري والدولار).
   - تكاليف التأسيس القانوني والتراخيص (مع احتساب إعفاءات Startup Qatar الأولى)، مساحة العمل المدعومة، مراجعة الأمن السيبراني، والتسويق الميداني.
6. **`6_CashFlow_and_Tranches` (التدفقات النقدية وتوزيع دفعات التمويل):**
   - رصيد النقدية أول كل شهر + **دفعات تمويل Startup Qatar (Tranche 1 / Tranche 2 / Tranche 3)** عند تحقق شهر الـ Milestone - المصروفات التشغيلية = الرصيد الختامي (`Runway` لا ينزل عن الصفر).
   - حساب نقطة التعادل التشغيلي (**Monthly Breakeven Point**) وإجمالي التمويل المطلوب حتى الوصول لها أو لجولة Series Seed.
